Our Services

Everything your garden needs, done properly.

Lawn Mowing

Regular cuts to keep your lawn looking neat, healthy and well maintained throughout the year. We work around your schedule and can set up regular weekly or fortnightly visits.

Hedge Trimming

Clean, precise trimming of hedges, bushes and borders. We'll shape them to your preference and clear up all the cuttings afterwards.

Garden Clearance

Full clearance of overgrown, neglected or end-of-season gardens. We remove weeds, cut back overgrowth, and leave the space clean and ready to use.

Regular Maintenance

Scheduled visits to keep your garden in top condition all year round. We tailor each visit to what your garden needs at that time of year.

Planting & Borders

Seasonal planting, border maintenance and weeding to keep your garden beds looking full and colourful.

One-Off Tidy Ups

Need a quick tidy before an event or just want a fresh start? We offer flexible one-off visits with no commitment required.
